# BMNRC Protocol Enforcement Check, 2026-10-02

What the validator code enforces for BMNRC, the first real NAVCoin on
`postfiat-wan-devnet-2`, and what the chain state shows.

- **Scope:** protocol enforcement only. This check does not assess the reserve composition, the valuation, the NAV, the price or hedge sources, the mandate, or any amount. It takes every number and source as given.
- **Code:** `c93b2137` (`release/combined-fastpay-20260928`). `crates/execution` and `crates/types` on `main` are identical to it.
- **Chain:** read-only RPC on validator-1, 10:58–11:03Z (`status`, `blocks`, `receipts`, `tx`, `batch_archive`, `asset_info`, `nav_reserve_proof_status`, `navcoin_bridge_routes`). No transaction was sent and no host was changed.
- **Paths:** `nft` is `crates/execution/src/nft_escrow_asset_execution.rs`, `vault` is `crates/execution/src/nav_vault_asset_execution.rs`, `sp1` is `crates/execution/src/nav_sp1_verifier.rs`, `res` is `crates/execution/src/vault_bridge_profile_resolution.rs`, `types` is `crates/types/src/market_nav_asset_types.rs`.
- **Prior review:** [NAVCoin specification platform review, 2026-10-01](navcoin-spec-platform-review-20261001.md) (R3, R22, R29, R33).

## Summary

1. BMNRC is bound to registered profile `4ff2afa5…`, of kind `sp1-nav-reserve-v1` with a Groth16 program key, so every packet is proof-checked while that binding holds.
2. Every validator verified the epoch-1 Groth16 proof when it executed `nav_reserve_submit` at 1099. A packet whose proof fails is rejected and not stored. Finalize does not verify the proof again.
3. The proof's public values bind the genesis, asset, profile, epoch, policy hash and source manifest. They do not bind the circulating supply, the NAV or the packet hash.
4. The exact N floor was enforced at epoch 1. It applies to the packet's declared supply (640585238), not to the ledger supply at finalize, which was 0.
5. Only the issuer may finalize or mint at NAV. The reserve operator may submit packets and operate the route.
6. **Not enforced:** nothing prevents the issuer from rebinding BMNRC to a profile without a proof check, and a route pause does not stop sells.
